## What the company does
Meta runs Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger and Threads, sells advertising against them, and is building AI models and infrastructure at enormous scale.

## Why it is on the list
Compounder bucket, rank 24. Forward P/E 17.5 vs a 24.2 median, 25% off its high. Cheap on earnings, but the cash flow behind those earnings has collapsed.

## Business quality
Distribution moat of 3 billion plus daily users and the best ad targeting outside Google. Revenue grew 28% last quarter. But net income fell 14% to $15.8 billion, operating margin dropped from 43% to 31%, and free cash flow shrank to $784 million from $8.55 billion as capex nearly doubled to $31.1 billion in one quarter.

## Valuation
17.5x forward looks cheap until you price free cash flow: at the current run rate the free cash flow yield is under 1%. Full year 2026 capex guidance is $135 to $145 billion. Until capex growth slows, the earnings multiple overstates what shareholders actually receive.

## Last two earnings reports
Q2 2026 (July 29, 2026): EPS $6.18 vs $7.14 estimate, capex guidance raised to $135 to $145 billion with the low end up $10 billion in a quarter, revenue forecast below expectations, stock down about 8% after hours. Q1 2026: also a capex driven reaction.

## The bear case
This is a company spending all of its cash flow, and then some, on AI infrastructure with no disclosed return timeline, while the core ad business faces the same AI attention shift as everyone else. The 2026 pattern of raising capex every quarter has no end date.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: capex guidance rising again in Q3, free cash flow negative for a full quarter, ad revenue growth under 10%. Price trigger: $450.

## Verdict
Watch. Not a buy until capex guidance stops rising. Watch zone $520 to $580. Conviction 2 of 5.
