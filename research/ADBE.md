## What the company does
Adobe sells subscription software for making and managing digital content: Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and the rest of Creative Cloud for creators, Acrobat and Express for everyone else, and Experience Cloud for marketing teams. Almost all revenue is recurring subscriptions.

## Why it is on the list
Compounder bucket. Rank 4 of 150 on quality (ROIC 31% average, 39% of revenue turns into free cash, share count down 9% over the window) while the stock is 59% below its all time high and trades near 10 to 12 times forward earnings against a four year median of 38. This is the cheapest a business of this quality has been in the data.

## Business quality
Moat is the file formats, the training and habits of every professional creative, and Acrobat as the default for PDFs. Management guided fiscal 2026 EPS to $24.35 to $24.45 and Q3 to $6.05 to $6.10, both above consensus, and ran a $25 billion buyback. Monthly active users across Acrobat, Creative Cloud, Express and Firefly reached 850 million, up 17% year over year. The CEO is leaving, which is a real management question mark. Insider ownership is small (0.2%).

## Valuation
At $285.75 with fiscal 2026 EPS guidance of about $24.40, the stock is about 11.7 times this year's earnings. Six month revenue to May 29, 2026 was $13.0 billion with $4.66 billion of operating income and $8.86 of diluted EPS; the company bought back $4.6 billion of stock in those six months. Cash $4.9 billion, debt $6.6 billion. Peers: Autodesk 16.7x forward, Microsoft 21.6x, ServiceNow 29x, Salesforce not in our universe screen. Three year view at $24.40 growing 8% a year gives roughly $30 to $31 of EPS in fiscal 2029. Bear: 10x on $27 (growth stalls, seat losses) is $270, roughly flat. Base: 15x on $30 is $450. Bull: 20x on $32 is $640. The asymmetry is what makes it a pick, not certainty about AI.

## The bear case
Generative image and video tools from OpenAI, Midjourney, Runway and Canva do 80% of what a prosumer needs for free or nearly free, and design tools inside AI chat products (Anthropic's Claude Design launched in April) push more of that base away every quarter. Per seat pricing is the wrong model for an agent era, so even if Adobe wins on product the pricing shrinks. Net new ARR has decelerated. The CEO exit removes the person who built the subscription transition. Cheap can stay cheap for years while the market waits for proof, and a 10x multiple on a business with real terminal value doubt is not obviously a floor.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: Digital Media ARR growth falling under 8%, two consecutive quarters of net new ARR decline, a cut to fiscal 2027 guidance below fiscal 2026, or a poorly received CEO appointment. Price trigger: a close under $220 without a company specific explanation means the market knows something the numbers do not show yet.

## Verdict
Buy, but after the September 10 report rather than before it. The print will either confirm the numbers or give a better price. Buy zone $250 to $300. Conviction 4 of 5.
