## What the company does
KLA makes the inspection and measurement tools that chip fabs use to find defects. Roughly 90% of revenue is process control, where it has a near monopoly; the rest is services and specialty equipment. It did a ten for one stock split in June 2026.

## Why it is on the list
Both buckets: rank 6 on quality (ROIC 39%, 30% free cash flow margin) and a cyclical turn. The stock is 43% off its high, forward P/E 25.9 vs a 35 median, and next year's EPS estimate rose 13% in 90 days. Guidance points to a record September quarter.

## Business quality
Process control intensity rises with every node and with advanced packaging, so KLA grows faster than wafer fab equipment overall. Gross margin 62.4%, operating margin 43.7%. China was about a third of fiscal 2025 revenue and export controls are a $300 to $350 million headwind this year.

## Valuation
At $172.94 and 25.9x forward. With September quarter EPS guided to $1.16, the run rate is about $4.60, so the stock is about 37x run rate but the estimates are climbing. Three year view: bear (2027 is the peak, revenue down 20% in 2028) 18x on $4 is $72, a real loss; base 24x on $7 is $168, flat to modest gains with the dividend; bull 28x on $9 is $250. This is why it is sized as a cyclical, not a compounder.

## Last two earnings reports
Q4 fiscal 2026 (July 28, 2026): revenue $3.66 billion up 15%, non-GAAP EPS $1.05 at the top of guidance, September quarter guided to $4.0 billion plus or minus $200 million and EPS $1.16. Shares fell on margin mix. Q3 fiscal 2026 (April) also beat with a pullback after.

## The bear case
Wafer fab equipment spending of $150 billion in 2026 is a peak fuelled by AI memory and logic capex; if memory capex slows in 2027 rather than shifts, orders and the multiple compress together. China restrictions tighten every year. Semi equipment stocks have fallen 50% or more in every prior downturn, and the group already rotated down 40% from the high once this year.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: December quarter guidance under $3.6 billion, calendar 2027 WFE outlook cut by 15% or more, or a new export rule that removes another 10% of revenue. Price trigger: $130.

## Verdict
Buy now, October tranche, sized as a cyclical. Buy zone $150 to $185. Conviction 4 of 5.
