## What the company does
Autodesk makes design and engineering software: AutoCAD, Revit for buildings, Fusion for manufacturing, Maya and 3ds Max for media. Architects, engineers and construction firms run their work on it, on subscription.

## Why it is on the list
Compounder bucket, rank 10. ROIC 23%, 30% free cash flow margin. Forward P/E 16.7 vs a 58.7 median, 29% off its high. The price collapsed with the software group while the business just raised guidance.

## Business quality
Standards moat: AutoCAD and Revit files are the language of the building industry and switching costs are careers, not licenses. The 10-K's own top risks are new product acceptance, competition and technology change, subscription renewal predictability, and concentration in AutoCAD based products. Insider ownership is small.

## The bear case
AI native design tools will generate drawings and models from prompts, and per seat CAD licenses shrink as fewer drafters are needed per project. Construction and manufacturing are cyclical and rates are rising again. The multiple already compressed from 59x to 17x in this data, and the market may be right that the terminal growth rate is lower than it was.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: billings growth under 5% for two quarters, fiscal 2028 guidance below fiscal 2027, or net revenue retention falling below 100%. Price trigger: $185.
