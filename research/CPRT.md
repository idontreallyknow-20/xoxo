## What the company does
Copart runs online auctions for damaged and salvage vehicles, mostly on behalf of insurers who total a car and need to sell it. It owns the yards, the towing network and the auction platform.

## Why it is on the list
Compounder bucket, rank 38. ROIC 19%, net cash, 24% free cash flow margin. Forward P/E 20 vs a 31.5 median, 50% off its all time high, down 38% over 52 weeks.

## Business quality
Duopoly with IAA/RB Global and the better yard footprint. The long term driver is total loss frequency rising as cars get more expensive to repair. The near term problem is insurance claim volumes: households dropped collision coverage or raised deductibles, so fewer cars reach auction. Insider ownership 9%, unusually high for a company this size.

## The bear case
Volumes are structurally lower if consumers keep dropping collision coverage, AI damage assessment lets insurers repair more cars instead of totaling them, and a competitor with a better platform takes share. Growth has stalled at 2% and the stock is still 20x.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: insurance unit declines accelerating past 8%, service revenue per unit falling, or a large insurer moving volume to IAA. Price trigger: $25.
