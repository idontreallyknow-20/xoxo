## What the company does
NVR builds homes under the Ryan Homes, NVHomes and Heartland brands in the eastern US and runs a mortgage bank for its buyers. It options land rather than owning it, which keeps the balance sheet light.

## Why it is on the list
Cyclical turn bucket, rank 147. 36% off its high, forward P/E 15.5 at its median. Orders grew 9% but margins compressed.

## Business quality
Best capital allocator in homebuilding: land light model, relentless buybacks ($358 million in one quarter, on a $17 billion company), no debt to speak of. Gross margin fell to 19.2% from 21.5% on lot costs, price cuts and $21.7 million of land deposit impairments.

## The bear case
Mortgage rates are heading up, not down, if the Fed hikes in September. Affordability is the binding constraint and NVR is cutting price to hold volume. Margins have further to fall.

## Key risks and thesis killers

## Verdict
