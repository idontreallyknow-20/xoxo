## Valuation
Cheap on trailing numbers. But the revenue forecast was cut, and a services firm at 13x with negative bookings growth is a value trap until bookings turn.

## Last two earnings reports
Q3 fiscal 2026 (June 18, 2026): revenue up 6% to $18.7 billion, EPS up 9% to $3.80, new bookings $19.3 billion down 2%, full year growth forecast lowered. Stock fell 18% to $128.46 on the day, a record decline. It has since recovered to $193, which itself argues against chasing.

## The bear case
AI does the work that Accenture bills for. Clients are pausing large programs while they figure out what to build in house. Bookings lead revenue by a year, and bookings are negative.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Bookings growth staying negative for another quarter, or another guidance cut.

## Verdict
Pass. Revisit if bookings growth turns positive for two quarters. Conviction 2 of 5.
