## What the company does
Booking Holdings runs Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, Kayak and OpenTable. It takes a commission or a merchant margin on hotel rooms, flights and rentals booked through its sites and apps, and is the largest online travel company in the world by bookings.

## Why it is on the list
Compounder bucket, rank 13. ROIC 43%, 34% free cash flow margin, share count down 19% over the window. Forward P/E 15.8 vs a 29.5 median. The stock is down about 24% this year on AI agent fears while the business posted record results.

## Business quality
Two sided network: the most hotel supply, especially in Europe and Asia, and the most direct traffic. Payments platform handles 100 plus methods and 50 plus currencies. The company returned $3.7 billion through buybacks in a single quarter plus a $0.42 dividend, with $14.5 billion of authorization remaining. Cash $17.2 billion against $20.2 billion of debt.

## Last two earnings reports
Q2 2026 (reported late July 2026): gross bookings $51.0 billion up 9%, revenue $7.4 billion up 8%, room nights 325 million up 5% (above the top of the outlook), adjusted EPS $2.54 up 15%, adjusted EBITDA $2.6 billion up 9%. Q3 outlook: room nights up 3 to 5%, revenue up 4 to 6%. Full year: high single digit revenue growth, low to mid teens EPS growth. Q1 2026 also beat with the same AI overhang on the stock.

## The bear case
AI agents will talk directly to hotel systems and a 15% commission will not survive that. Growth is already slowing to mid single digits in room nights, the Middle East conflict is hurting travel demand, and Google keeps pushing its own hotel booking. If the take rate compresses by even two points, earnings fall 15%.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: two consecutive quarters of negative room nights, take rate falling more than 100 basis points year over year, or buybacks pausing. Price trigger: $150. Note that in March 2026 OpenAI pulled checkout from ChatGPT because users would not enter cards there, and the stock rose 8% that day, which is evidence against the bear case, not proof.
