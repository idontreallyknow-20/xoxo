## What the company does
Alphabet is Google: search and search advertising, YouTube, Android, Chrome, Google Cloud, and the Gemini AI models, plus Waymo and other bets.

## Why it is on the list
Compounder bucket, rank 32. Forward P/E 23.1, right at its four year median. Not cheap versus its own history, but revenue grew 24% last quarter with Cloud up 82% and a $514 billion Cloud backlog. It is on the list for quality and growth, with the entry point being the question.

## Business quality
Search is still a 90% share product and grew 17% last quarter to $63.3 billion despite AI Overviews and chat competitors. Cloud has become a second engine: $24.8 billion of quarterly revenue with operating income of $8.8 billion versus $2.8 billion a year earlier. The December 2025 antitrust judgment left Chrome and Android intact but imposed data sharing and default restrictions. Insider ownership is about 1.6%.

## Valuation
At $342 and 23.1x forward the stock sits at its own median and above Microsoft and Meta. Three year view with 15% EPS growth: bear 18x on trough growth is roughly $330, flat; base 22x is about $520; bull 26x is about $620. The upside is real but the entry is average, so it is a pullback buy.

## Last two earnings reports
Q2 2026 (July 22, 2026): revenue $119.8 billion vs $117.0 billion consensus, up 24%; Cloud up 82%; EPS $9.11. Shares fell 3.7% after hours on capex concerns. Q1 2026 showed a similar beat with Cloud acceleration and a muted reaction.

## The bear case
Every dollar of AI capex (roughly $180 billion) is a dollar the market once counted as free cash flow, and the search franchise faces a slow erosion from choice screens, Apple renegotiations, EU data sharing, and users starting queries in chat products instead of Google. The adtech remedies ruling could force an AdX divestiture. Cloud growth at 82% cannot last and will decelerate into a lower multiple.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: search revenue growth under 5% for two quarters, an adtech ruling that breaks up the ad stack, or Cloud operating margin reversing. Price trigger: a close under $260.

## Verdict
Buy on pullback, November tranche or earlier if the zone is hit. Buy zone $300 to $335. Conviction 3 of 5.
