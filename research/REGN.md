## What the company does
Regeneron is a biotech with two franchises: Dupixent (with Sanofi) for asthma, eczema and other inflammatory diseases, and Eylea for eye disease, plus a deep pipeline and a genetics research engine.

## Why it is on the list
Compounder bucket, rank 69. Forward P/E 13.9 vs an 18.7 median, 29% off its high. Two straight quarters of double digit growth on both lines while the market fixates on Eylea biosimilars.

## Business quality
Dupixent is one of the largest drugs in the world and still growing 38%: $6 billion of quarterly global sales across nine indications. Eylea HD is converting the base (up 52% to $596 million in the US) while legacy Eylea declines 45% to biosimilars. Net cash balance sheet. Science driven management with a long record.

## The bear case
Eylea was half the company two years ago and it is being eaten by biosimilars faster than HD can replace it. Dupixent growth will decelerate from 38% as indications mature, and Sanofi takes a large share of it. The pipeline has had failures and drug pricing politics are a permanent overhang.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: Dupixent growth under 15%, Eylea HD sales down sequentially for two quarters, or a pricing law that hits Dupixent specifically. Price trigger: $650.
