## What the company does
ServiceNow sells a workflow platform that large companies use to run IT service desks, HR, customer service and security operations, increasingly with AI agents doing the routing and resolution.

## Why it is on the list
Compounder bucket, rank 63. Subscription revenue growing 24.5%, forward P/E 29 vs a 123 median, 42% off its high. Still the most expensive name on the shortlist on earnings.

## Business quality
Platform moat inside the enterprise: once workflows are built on it they rarely leave. ServiceNow AI passed $1 billion of annual contract value with agentic deployments up ninefold in nine months. Margins are under some pressure from AI compute costs.

At $145.59 and 29x forward, growth of 22% gives a PEG near 1.3, fair rather than cheap. Three year view: bear 22x is roughly $150, flat; base 30x on 20% compounded EPS is about $250; bull 38x is about $320.

## Last two earnings reports
Q2 2026 (July 22, 2026): subscription revenue $3.877 billion up 24.5%, full year subscription guidance raised to $15.76 to $15.78 billion. Shares fell 6.5% intraday then recovered after hours. Q1 2026 (April 23) triggered a sector wide plunge alongside IBM as investors read AI as a threat rather than a driver.

## The bear case
Agents that live in the model layer make the workflow layer a commodity: why route a ticket through ServiceNow when the assistant resolves it? Seat based pricing shrinks as agents replace people. Margins are already compressing from AI costs, and at 29x there is no valuation cushion if growth slips to the teens.

## Key risks and thesis killers
Thesis killers: subscription growth under 18%, operating margin guidance cut, or a large customer publicly replacing it with an agent platform. Price trigger: $110.

## Verdict
Buy on pullback only. Buy zone $115 to $135. Not in the initial portfolio; ADBE and ADSK carry the software exposure at better prices. Conviction 3 of 5.
